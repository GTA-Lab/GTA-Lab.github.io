[Dr. Faezeh Rezvanifard](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=lQO4L9YAAAAJ&hl=en) is an Assistant Professor of Mathematics Education in the Department of Applied Mathematics, Faculty of Mathematical Sciences at Ferdowsi University of Mashhad. Her research lies at the intersection of applied mathematics and didactics, focusing on the teaching and learning of university-level mathematics and instructional task design. Her work contributes to bridging the conceptual and curricular gaps between introductory mathematics courses and technical engineering curricula through the development of instructional reference models, as well as the integration of mathematical modeling and puzzle-based problem-solving.
Currently, she is focused on designing meaningful pedagogical approaches to graph theory education for mathematics and computer science students to enhance conceptual understanding and academic performance. Her research in this area encompasses analyzing textbooks, investigating students’ problem-solving practices across diverse graph-theoretic tasks, and developing evidence-based instructional recommendations to advance students’ learning.
